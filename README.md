# Rospuek

Rospuek is an AI operator and agent orchestration platform built by Zach Edwards LLC. It coordinates specialized AI agents, tools, models, browser sessions, and business systems from a single operator interface, so that complex, multi-step digital work can be planned, delegated, reviewed, and completed.

Website: https://rospuek.zachedwardsllc.com

## What is Rospuek?

Rospuek is an AI operator. Rather than answering questions in a single chat, it runs work: it breaks a task into pieces, hands each piece to a specialized agent, checks the results, and reports back. Its tagline is "Rospuek does what you do" - custom AI technology shaped around the work already on someone's desk, rather than a general-purpose chatbot.

## Who makes Rospuek?

Rospuek is made by Zach Edwards LLC, a software company based in Idaho, United States.

## What can Rospuek do?

- Orchestrate and delegate work across multiple specialized AI agents
- Research using real, logged-in browser sessions rather than search snippets alone
- Find and qualify business leads, and prepare outreach
- Design, build, test, and ship websites and software
- Generate images, video, music, voice, and 3D assets
- Quote manufacturing work from 3D CAD files
- Track meals, training, and goals, and coach by text message
- Study scripture alongside a dedicated agent

## How is Rospuek different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers inside one conversation. Rospuek runs many agents at once against real systems - browsers, files, APIs, scheduled jobs - and verifies the results before reporting. Work continues in the background rather than ending when a reply is sent.

## Who is it for?

Small businesses and independent operators who need software, a website, lead generation, or automation, but do not have a technical team.
