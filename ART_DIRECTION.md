# Zach's Dream Journal — Artwork Direction

## LOCKED PRIMARY REFERENCE: Style 2, the mysterious staircase

Approved by the journal owner on 2026-11-23. The reference is the **middle panel** of the three-part image set comparing (1) a soft floral shepherd scene, (2) a mysterious luminous staircase, and (3) a darker intimate embrace. The selected second style is a visual LANGUAGE, not an instruction to add literal staircases to every dream.

### Essential qualities
- Fine-art, painterly, atmospheric, mysterious, spiritually contemplative.
- Thick, irregular oil-paint / palette-knife texture, visible brushstrokes, imperfect handmade edges. Objects fade into painted atmosphere.
- Dramatic yet restrained chiaroscuro: deep blue-gray, charcoal, muted teal, smoky umber, ash, and subtle warm cream / ivory light.
- Light emerges through layers of fog and imperfect brush marks, diffused and softly luminous rather than laser beams or glossy halos.
- Strong sense of verticality, depth, mystery, solitude, scale, and sacred wonder. Allow substantial negative space; human figures can be tiny or indistinct.
- A balance of abstraction and recognizable imagery: distinct enough to tell the scripture or dream, but not every detail sharply resolved.
- Make emotionally specific compositions, not generic religious posters or stock-fantasy illustrations. Varied subjects and palettes are permitted while maintaining the material, lighting, and painterly atmosphere.

### Avoid
- Glossy cinematic fantasy concept art, photorealism, HDR hyper-detail, AI-poster excess.
- Giant ornate heavenly palaces, gratuitous sunbeams, overcrowded scenes, stock religious motifs.
- Uniformly golden skies, highly saturated flowers, over-smooth skin and fabric, perfectly delineated buildings.
- Literal staircases unless a dream or scripture actually calls for them.
- Text, quotes, logos, or watermarks printed within the artwork.

### Secondary references
- Soft pink impressionistic shepherd and lamb scene: approved for warmer, gentle dreams and floral passages.
- Dark embrace in the third panel: can inform intimate, painful, or redemptive scenes, but is not the primary style.

### Creative workflow
1. Read the source scripture or approved dream transcript; record key symbols and emotional tone.
2. Sketch composition in prose: scale, focal point, foreground/background, use of negative space, lighting.
3. Create 1–3 distinct concepts honoring Style 2's atmosphere, not necessarily its exact palette.
4. User critiques; refine the selected concept.
5. Preserve final art along with its scripture/dream reference and editorial approval.
